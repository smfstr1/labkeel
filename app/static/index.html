Phone app

Put the dashboard on your phone's home screen, with its own icon. It opens full-screen, like an app.

Any picture works: it's cropped to a square and made 512 pixels across. Keep the important part in the middle, since phones round the corners.

iPhone or iPad: open the dashboard in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen (or Install app). After changing the icon, remove the old one from your home screen and add it again.

Container updates

Look for newer versions of the images your containers run. Nothing is downloaded or changed: you choose when to update.

LabKeel asks each image's registry (Docker Hub, GHCR and others) which version its tag points to now: one small request per image, which doesn't count towards Docker Hub's download limit. Images that need a login to read are skipped. To be told about new versions, tick it under Alerts.

Warning limits

Scrubs and self-tests

A scrub reads every block and repairs anything that has quietly gone wrong. Set 0 to turn the reminder off.

Drives test themselves in the background and keep working meanwhile. A short test takes a couple of minutes; a long one reads the whole drive and can take hours on a big hard drive. You get an alert if one fails.

Alerts

Get a notification on your phone or in Discord when something needs you. Each problem is sent once, and again when it's fixed.

Quiet hours

Alerts that can wait arrive as one summary when quiet hours end. A failing drive, a degraded pool or repeated wrong passwords still get through straight away.

Power estimate

The dashboard adds up what the CPU, memory and graphics cards report about themselves. Drives, fans and the power supply's own losses can't be measured from software, so add an allowance for them here.

Electricity price per kWh

With a smart plug, compare its reading with the Overview figure and put the difference in "Rest of the machine". Both settings apply to past history too. Leave the price empty to hide costs.

Weekly Docker clean-up

Remove images no container is using, plus old build files, so they don't slowly fill the system drive. Anything removed downloads again when it's next needed.
